### Research: why a fresh New Game shows the doubled ocarina and the natural save doesn't

Static trace by cs21c on the dump (1.1.1, v131072).

**What the farewell reads.** `Dm_Kokiri_0155` has no switch event: it reads no GameData, pouch or equipment
state. Saria's own ocarina there is the prop `Dm_Item_Ocarina_Fairy_Saria` (create method 1), bound to bridge
Saria's `Weapon_R` with `RotOffset (180, 0, 0)` at Event7, when she draws it. Bridge Saria (placement
`KokiriForest_ArriveLostWood`, Hash `1660754835063030086`) is created by the event's own Bool
(`rounds/r14-steps.md:72-76`); in `0155` she is participant 6 (create method 2) with every `EquipmentUser_*` slot
blank. So whatever differs between a glitching and a clean farewell comes from Saria's actor state when she
appears, not from the flow.

**Saria carries an ocarina of her own.** `Npc_Kokiri_Saria_Child` uses `EquipmentUserRef` ->
`EquipmentUserParam/Npc_Hylia_Ocarina`, whose blackboard table defaults `EquipmentUser_Accessory1` to
`Npc_Equipment_009`, with `AccessoryBindBoneName1 = Weapon_R` (`EquipmentUserParam/Default`). `Npc_Equipment_009`
shares the Fairy Ocarina's model project (`plans/chest-ocarina-model.md:243`).

**Her AI schedule decides whether she holds it.** `Component/AIScheduleParam/Npc_Kokiri_Saria_Child` lists
conditional schedules in story order: `KokiriForest_LinkHouseGoDown`, `HavePlayedEvent.Dm_Kokiri_0060`,
